SMS Opt-In

Quantum Notify · Last updated November 4, 2026

This page documents how a recipient opts in to SMS notifications from Quantum Notify. There is exactly one recipient: the account owner.

Call to action

Text START to the Quantum Notify SMS number

Sending that message is the opt-in. No messages are sent to a number that has not texted first.

Consent language

By texting START to the Quantum Notify SMS number you agree to receive recurring SMS notifications from Quantum Notify: server and agent status alerts, task results, and replies to questions you send by text. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out at any time.

What happens after START

  1. You receive a confirmation: "Quantum Notify: you are subscribed to account and server notifications from Quantum Notify. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
  2. You reply YES to complete the double opt-in.
  3. Notifications and replies are then delivered to that number only.

Opting out

Reply STOP (or CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL) at any time. You receive one final message confirming the unsubscribe, and nothing further unless you text START again.
